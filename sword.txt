Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj’s legendary sword is known as the Bhavani Talwar. Named after Goddess Bhavani—the patron deity of the Maratha Empire—tradition holds that the sword was blessed to give him victory in battle.A masterpiece of 17th-century craftsmanship, this double-edged blade featured an intricate hilt and symbolized bravery, justice, and the defense of Swarajya (self-rule). To this day, it remains one of the most revered symbols of Maratha warrior heritage.
