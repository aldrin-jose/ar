Anizham Thirunal Marthanda Varma, the founder of the modern kingdom of Travancore, famously used the Chera Mudi an ancient crown of the Chera dynast when assuming the imperial title of Kulasekhara Perumal.  However, the defining moment regarding his crown occurred in January 1750 during the sacred act of Thrippadidanam.Marthanda Varma placed his royal crown, sword, and entire kingdom at the lotus feet of the deity at the Sree Padmanabhaswamy Temple in Thiruvananthapuram.By surrendering his crown, he renounced absolute earthly kingship, declaring that Lord Padmanabha was the true ruler of Travancore. From that point on, he and his successors ruled not as sovereign monarchs wearing a worldly crown, but as humble Padmanabha Dasas (servants of the Lord).
