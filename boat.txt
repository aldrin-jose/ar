This is a Vanchi or Thoni, a traditional wooden country boat used across Kerala's backwaters and rivers. Handcrafted from local timber like Anjili or teak, its narrow, double-ended hull is built to glide effortlessly through shallow canals. Operated using a single long wooden oar or bamboo pole (Kazhukol), these timeless vessels have served local fishermen and villagers for centuries as an essential mode of daily transport, fishing, and living alongside the water.
